Broad knowledge is the thing small models trade away

Small Language Models (SLMs) achieve their impressive speed and local-execution capabilities through a ruthless editing process. They optimize heavily for specific functional tasks like document summarization, code generation, or structured data extraction.

To fit inside restricted memory footprints—like a smartphone's RAM—these models discard the vast, sprawling web of general trivia, historical tangents, and creative writing data that bulk up their larger counterparts. This makes them highly efficient tools, but inherently limited in their world scope.

It is an intentional, calculated trade-off for efficiency.
