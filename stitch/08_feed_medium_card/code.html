Small models are eating the easy jobs

While the industry obsesses over massive, billion-parameter artificial intelligence models, a quiet revolution is happening at the edge. Small language models (SLMs), designed to run locally on-device, are increasingly taking over narrow, specialized tasks.

Because they don't require round-trips to a cloud server, these models offer significantly reduced latency and operating costs. They aren't meant to write poetry or complex code; instead, they excel at fast, deterministic categorization and processing.

1 Real-time on-device audio transcription.
2 Contextual smart-reply generation in mobile keyboards.
3 Initial document triage and classification.

Narrow work is cheap work.
