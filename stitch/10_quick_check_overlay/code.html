The Rise of Small Language Models

850 WORDS

While massive foundational models dominate the headlines, a quieter revolution is happening in the world of Small Language Models (SLMs). These efficient, narrowly focused networks are proving that bigger isn't always better when context is constrained.

By limiting parameter count and fine-tuning on specific datasets, SLMs can run locally on consumer hardware, dramatically reducing inference costs and latency while preserving privacy.
